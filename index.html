6 Key Decision Roles
ROLE 01

Final Decision-Maker

Approves or rejects the investment, supplier or strategic direction.

ROLE 02

Budget Owner

Controls or allocates the relevant budget and capital expenditure.

ROLE 03

Evaluator

Assesses products, vendors, technologies, capabilities or services.

ROLE 04

Recommender

Builds the vendor shortlist or internal formal recommendation.

ROLE 05

Influencer

Shapes requirements or contributes specialist domain expertise.

ROLE 06

End User / Implementer

Uses, deploys, integrates or manages the selected solution daily.
